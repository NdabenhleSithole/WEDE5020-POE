About Us

Learn more about SHEIN and its global retail platform.

Our History

SHEIN is a global online fashion and lifestyle retailer founded in 2012. The organisation developed as a digital-first retailer, using an online platform to connect customers with a large and frequently changing selection of products.

Today, SHEIN serves customers internationally and operates a dedicated South African shopping platform with products and pricing relevant to local shoppers.

Mission

To make fashion accessible to a broad range of customers by providing an extensive online selection and a convenient digital shopping experience.

Vision

To continue developing a customer-focused global fashion and lifestyle platform that responds quickly to changing consumer preferences.
